MEDICAL INFLATION REALITY

Why Adequate Health Coverage Is Essential

Healthcare expenses in India have witnessed consistent double-digit inflation over recent years. Advanced surgical procedures, specialty ICU care, and modern diagnostics can quickly drain family savings without structured insurance protection.

A well-structured health insurance policy acts as an indemnity buffer, enabling timely admission to quality private healthcare facilities without liquidating mutual funds, fixed deposits, or taking personal debt during a medical emergency.

COVERAGE FORMATS

Health Insurance Categories

Selecting the appropriate policy construct based on household dependents and age profiles.

Solo Cover

Individual Health Insurance

Dedicated sum insured reserved exclusively for a single policyholder. Ideal for young working professionals looking to build early policy history and bypass waiting periods.

Shared Cover

Family Floater Health Insurance

A single umbrella sum insured protecting self, spouse, and dependent children. Offers cost efficiency and pooled coverage for young nuclear households.

Elder Protection

Senior Citizen Health Insurance

Tailored specifically for parents and elders above 60 years. Focuses on pre-existing condition management, reduced waiting periods, and critical illness support.

Enhanced Security

Health Protection Planning

Strategic combination of base indemnity policies with Super Top-Up plans and defined-benefit Critical Illness covers to achieve high sum insured (e.g. ₹50L–₹1 Cr) cost-effectively.

POLICY BUYER'S GUIDE

What To Consider Before Choosing A Health Policy

An educational checklist to ensure you evaluate terms thoroughly before committing to an insurer.

01. Coverage Scope

Inpatient & Daycare

Check whether modern daycare treatments (dialysis, cataract, chemotherapy) are covered without 24h admission.

02. Pricing Structure

Premium vs. Age Bands

Understand how premiums step up across age brackets so renewals remain affordable in future decades.

03. Waiting Periods

Pre-Existing Diseases

Verify waiting period terms (typically 24–48 months) for existing ailments like hypertension, thyroid, or diabetes.

04. Exclusions

What Is Not Covered

Review permanent exclusions such as cosmetic treatments, unproven therapies, and initial 30-day waiting limits.

05. Network Hospitals

Local Cashless Empanelment

Confirm that premier hospitals in your city/locality have active cashless empanelment with the chosen insurer.

06. Co-payments

Mandatory Out-Of-Pocket

Check whether the plan requires you to pay 10%–20% of every approved claim out-of-pocket, common in senior policies.

07. Room-Rent Limits

Sub-limits & Proportionate Cuts

Ensure the policy has 'No Room Rent Capping' to avoid proportional deductions across doctor fees and surgeries.

08. Policy Terms

Restoration & No Claim Bonus

Review automatic sum insured recharge benefits and cumulative bonus acceleration upon healthy claim-free years.
