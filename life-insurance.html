Life Insurance Categories

Understanding different structures helps match coverage with your financial objectives.

Pure Protection

Term Insurance

Provides substantial financial cover for a defined tenure at low, fixed premiums. In the event of the insured's demise during the term, the entire sum assured is disbursed to beneficiaries.

  • • High sum assured at affordable cost
  • • Optional critical illness riders
  • • Fixed tenure up to retirement
Lifetime Security

Life Protection

Comprehensive life policies designed to extend coverage over long horizons, ensuring permanent protection for surviving family members and estate continuity.

  • • Extended protection horizons
  • • Estate & legacy preservation
  • • Dependent maintenance
Disciplined Savings

Savings-Oriented Plans

Insurance vehicles combining risk cover with systematic premium savings. Designed to foster disciplined accumulation for predetermined future dates.

  • • Structured milestone pay-outs
  • • Contractual terms and transparency

Long-Term Financial Planning

KNOWLEDGE & CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Important answers to help you evaluate life and term insurance effectively.

Life insurance is a contract between an individual and an insurance provider. In exchange for regular premium payments, the insurer provides a pre-agreed sum assured to the designated beneficiaries in the event of the insured person's death during the policy period.
Any individual with dependents—such as aging parents, a spouse, or children—or outstanding financial liabilities like home loans, car loans, or business credit lines should consider life insurance. It ensures debt and lifestyle obligations do not burden dependents unexpectedly.
A general rule of thumb suggests a sum assured of at least 10 to 15 times your annual income, plus the outstanding balance of any existing debts (e.g. home loan), minus current liquid assets. Our advisors help you calculate this precisely based on your living expenses and family horizon.
Term insurance is the purest form of life insurance. It provides financial coverage for a fixed tenure (e.g., 20, 30, or 40 years). Because it does not incorporate an investment or cash-value component, term insurance offers the highest sum assured at the most economical premium rates.
Key factors include your entry age, tobacco/smoking status, current health profile, medical history, family health background, occupation risk level, chosen policy term, and selected sum assured.
Standard requirements typically include: Government ID proof (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport), Address proof (Passport / Utility bill / Aadhaar), Age proof, Income proof (recent 3 months' salary slips, 6 months' bank statements, or latest 2-3 years' ITR / Form 16), and recent passport-size photographs. Medical tests may also be conducted based on age and cover amount.
Yes. Most insurers offer optional riders such as Critical Illness Benefit, Accidental Death Benefit, Permanent Total Disability Rider, and Waiver of Premium upon critical illness or disability. These riders provide additional targeted security at a modest incremental premium.
