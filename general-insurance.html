VEHICULAR PROTECTION

Motor Insurance

From private luxury cars to daily two-wheelers and commercial logistics vehicles, motor insurance protects against road accidents, third-party legal liabilities, theft, and natural disasters.

Car Insurance (Private Four-Wheelers)

Comprehensive policies with essential add-on riders like Zero-Depreciation (Bumper-to-Bumper), Engine & Gearbox Protector, Return to Invoice, Roadside Assistance, and Consumables cover.

Two-Wheeler Insurance

Economical multi-year third-party liability and comprehensive own-damage covers for scooters and motorcycles, protecting against theft and accidental collisions.

Commercial Vehicle Insurance

Mandatory regulatory coverage for goods-carrying transport, passenger taxis, buses, and institutional fleets to prevent enterprise liability.

RESIDENTIAL & COMMERCIAL

Property & Business Protection

Property Protection

Shield your residential property—structure and internal contents—against perils including accidental fire, lightning, earthquake, inundation, burst pipes, and burglary.

Business Protection

Shield business enterprises, warehouses, factories, retail outlets, and offices from operational disruptions, machinery breakdown, and third-party legal claims.
