Insurance Solutions For Different Stages Of Life

Your insurance priorities evolve as your family, career, and asset base expand. Explore curated coverage roadmaps aligned with where you stand today.

PORTFOLIO ALIGNMENT

Where Are You On Your Financial Journey?

Every life transition brings new responsibilities. We structure protective shields around your immediate risks.

Stage 01

Young Professionals

Early in your career, lock in low premium rates for life and health insurance while pre-existing condition exclusions are minimal.

Recommended Coverage:
  • • Affordable term life protection
  • • Individual base health insurance (₹10L–₹25L)
  • • Personal accident & disability rider
Stage 02

Married Couples

Unite your household financial protection. Secure joint liabilities, home mortgages, and consolidate healthcare covers.

Recommended Coverage:
  • • Dual-earner term life insurance
  • • Comprehensive family floater health plan
  • • Home contents & structural property cover
Stage 03

Parents & Families

Provide unwavering financial security so children's school, college, and future ambitions remain uncompromised through any eventuality.

Recommended Coverage:
  • • High sum assured term plan (15-20x income)
  • • Family floater + Super Top-up (₹50L–₹1 Cr)
  • • Long-term financial planning & education cover
Stage 04

Senior Citizens (60+)

Transition focus towards medical indemnity, critical illness buffers, and manageable room-rent sub-limits for aging parents.

Recommended Coverage:
  • • Senior citizen health insurance
  • • Critical illness lump sum benefit cover
  • • Guaranteed lifetime annuity / pension reviews
Stage 05

Business Owners

Protect enterprise balance sheets, commercial premises, factory machinery, and key operational liabilities from disruption.

Recommended Coverage:
  • • Commercial property & Bharat Udyam covers
  • • Commercial General Liability (CGL)
  • • Keyman insurance & group health for staff
Stage 06

Vehicle Owners

Safeguard your personal four-wheelers or fleet with zero-depreciation add-ons, invoice return, and seamless cashless claims.

Recommended Coverage:
  • • Bumper-to-Bumper zero depreciation
  • • Engine protection & consumables cover
  • • 24x7 Roadside Assistance & towing
